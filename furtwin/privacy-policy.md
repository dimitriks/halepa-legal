# Privacy Policy for FurTwin

**Last updated: October 28, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

FurTwin is a mobile application developed by Halepa ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how FurTwin handles your information.

FurTwin lets you compare a photograph of a person with a photograph of a dog or cat to generate a fun resemblance result. The app also provides options to save and share results and offers an optional one-time Premium upgrade.

**FurTwin processes photographs locally on your device. We do not upload your photographs to our servers or use cloud-based AI services to analyze them.**

## 2. Information We Collect

FurTwin does not require account registration and does not collect personal information through its photo comparison features.

We do not independently collect:

- Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
- Account credentials
- Contacts or messages
- Precise location information
- Advertising identifiers
- Photographs or facial images on our servers
- Analytics or advertising data

FurTwin does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

For Premium purchases, FurTwin communicates with Google Play Billing to obtain purchase and entitlement information, as described in Section 5.

## 3. Photographs and Image Processing

You can choose photographs from your device's photo library or take new photographs using your device's camera.

Photographs are used to detect and compare a person and a pet and generate a resemblance result.

All image analysis and AI model inference are performed locally on your device.

We do not transmit your photographs, extracted image features, or comparison results to FurTwin servers or third-party AI services for analysis.

### Temporary Photo Storage

On Android, the operating system and photo selection components may create temporary copies of selected or captured photographs in FurTwin's private application cache.

These copies remain on your device and may include metadata from the original photographs, such as location metadata embedded in the image. FurTwin does not independently read or use this location information.

Removing a photograph from FurTwin's interface clears it from the active comparison but does not necessarily delete its cached file immediately.

Temporary files may remain until Android clears the application cache, you manually clear the cache, or you uninstall the application.

Cached photographs are excluded from Android Auto Backup.

## 4. App Preferences, Premium Status, and Device Backups

FurTwin stores sound and vibration preferences locally in the application's private storage.

To support Premium access, FurTwin may also store a local record of the purchased product identifier and whether Premium features are unlocked. Payment card details, purchase tokens, and Google account email addresses are not stored in FurTwin's local Premium cache.

The app may store temporary information needed to recover an interrupted Android photo selection flow. This recovery information does not contain the photograph itself or its file path.

Depending on your Android device settings, locally stored preferences and photo selection recovery information may be included in backups managed by the Android operating system or your device's backup provider.

The local Premium cache and temporary photograph files are excluded from Android Auto Backup.

These backups are controlled by the operating system and the relevant backup service, not by FurTwin.

FurTwin does not operate its own cloud backup service.

## 5. Google Play Billing and Third-Party Services

### Premium Purchases

FurTwin offers optional Premium features through a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play.

Google Play handles payment processing. FurTwin does not directly receive or store your payment card number or billing credentials.

To activate or restore Premium access, FurTwin communicates with Google Play Billing and processes information such as the purchased product identifier, purchase status, and purchase token.

Purchase tokens are used during purchase processing and are not stored in FurTwin's local Premium cache or sent to FurTwin-operated servers for verification.

Google Play may independently process purchase and payment information under Google's own privacy policies.

### Device and Operating-System Services

FurTwin uses operating-system features to let you select photographs, open your device's camera, save images, and share results.

Your device's camera application, photo library, cloud photo provider, and operating-system backup services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

FurTwin does not control the independent data practices of these services.

The application's image processing models are included with the app and run locally on your device.

## 6. Saving and Sharing Results

FurTwin allows you to create images containing your comparison results, including selected photographs, similarity scores, and optional visual layouts.

When you choose to save a result, FurTwin writes the exported image to your device's photo storage, typically under **Pictures/FurTwin** on Android.

When you choose to share a result, FurTwin prepares an image file and opens Android's system sharing interface. The image is sent to a selected application or recipient only when you initiate the sharing action.

The receiving application or service may process, store, or transmit the shared image according to its own privacy policy.

FurTwin does not automatically upload saved or shared results to its own servers.

Temporary export files may remain in the application's cache until cleared by Android or the user.

## 7. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your photographs or personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

FurTwin does not send photographs, extracted image features, or comparison results to FurTwin-operated servers.

Information needed to process or restore Premium purchases is handled through Google Play Billing.

If you choose to share a result, the exported image is provided to the application or recipient you select through Android's sharing interface.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

FurTwin does not maintain user accounts or store your photographs on FurTwin-operated servers.

Photograph copies and temporary exported images may remain in your device's private application cache after you remove photographs from the application's interface or finish sharing.

You can clear FurTwin's cache through your Android device settings.

Locally stored preferences, photo selection recovery information, and Premium status data can be removed by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling FurTwin.

Images you explicitly save to your device's photo library, including those in **Pictures/FurTwin**, are stored outside FurTwin's private application data and may remain after uninstalling the app. You can delete these images through your device's gallery or file manager.

Copies of locally stored data held in operating-system backups are subject to the relevant backup provider's retention and deletion controls.

Google Play independently retains purchase-related records according to its own policies.

## 9. Children's Privacy

FurTwin is intended for users aged 13 and older.

FurTwin does not require registration and does not intentionally collect personal information from users, including teenagers, through its photo comparison features.

If you have questions or concerns about privacy, you can contact us using the email address below.

## 10. Security

FurTwin is designed to process photographs locally, without transmitting them to FurTwin-operated servers for analysis.

Temporary photographs, exported images, and application preferences stored in the application's private Android storage areas are protected by the operating system's application sandbox.

Images explicitly saved to shared device storage or sent to other applications are subject to the security and privacy protections of those locations or services.

No method of storage or security protection can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

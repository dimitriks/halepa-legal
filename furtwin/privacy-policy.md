# Privacy Policy for FurTwin

**Last updated: October 12, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

FurTwin is a mobile application developed by Halepa ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how FurTwin handles your information.

FurTwin lets you compare a photograph of a person with a photograph of a dog or cat to generate a fun resemblance result.

**FurTwin processes photographs locally on your device. We do not upload your photographs to our servers or use cloud-based AI services to analyze them.**

## 2. Information We Collect

FurTwin does not require account registration and does not collect personal information through its application features.

We do not collect:

- Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
- Account credentials
- Contacts or messages
- Precise location information
- Advertising identifiers
- Photographs or facial images on our servers
- Analytics or advertising data

FurTwin does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

## 3. Photographs and Image Processing

You can choose photographs from your device's photo library or take new photographs using your device's camera.

Photographs are used to detect and compare a person and a pet and generate a resemblance result.

All image analysis and AI model inference are performed locally on your device.

We do not transmit your photographs, extracted image features, or comparison results to FurTwin servers or third-party AI services.

### Temporary Photo Storage

On Android, the operating system and photo selection components may create temporary copies of selected or captured photographs in FurTwin's private application cache.

These copies remain on your device and may include metadata from the original photographs, such as location metadata embedded in the image. FurTwin does not independently read or use this location information.

Removing a photograph from FurTwin's interface clears it from the active comparison, but does not necessarily delete its cached file immediately.

Temporary files may remain until Android clears the application cache, you manually clear the cache, or you uninstall the application.

Cached photographs are excluded from Android Auto Backup.

## 4. App Preferences and Device Backups

FurTwin stores your sound and vibration preferences locally in the application's private storage.

Depending on your Android device settings, these preferences may be included in backups managed by the Android operating system or your device's backup provider.

These backups are controlled by the operating system and the relevant backup service, not by FurTwin.

FurTwin does not operate its own cloud backup service.

## 5. Third-Party Services and Device Features

FurTwin uses operating-system features to let you select photographs or open your device's camera.

Your device's camera application, photo library, cloud photo provider, and operating-system backup services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

FurTwin does not control the independent data practices of these services.

The application's image processing models are included with the app and run locally on your device.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your photographs or personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or other companies through FurTwin's application features.

We do not send photographs or comparison results to external servers.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

FurTwin does not maintain user accounts or store your photographs on our servers.

Photograph copies may remain in your device's private application cache after you remove them from the application's interface.

You can clear FurTwin's cache through your Android device settings. Uninstalling FurTwin removes its application data from the device, subject to applicable operating-system backup behavior.

Locally stored preferences may be removed when you clear the application's storage or uninstall it. Copies of preferences held in operating-system backups are subject to the backup provider's retention and deletion controls.

## 8. Children's Privacy

FurTwin is intended for users aged 13 and older.

FurTwin does not require registration and does not intentionally collect personal information from users, including teenagers, through its application features.

If you have questions or concerns about privacy, you can contact us using the email address below.

## 9. Security

FurTwin is designed to process photographs locally, without transmitting them to FurTwin servers.

Temporary photographs and application preferences are stored within the application's private Android storage areas, which are protected by the operating system's application sandbox.

No method of storage or security protection can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to FurTwin, its features, or applicable legal requirements.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
